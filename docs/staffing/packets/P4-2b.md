  D216 doctrine, for the exact semantics: "every subscription channel carries a named
  `reserve_fraction` (default 0.10; Anthropic and Gemini explicitly 0.10) in `policy.yaml`.
  When a channel's remaining fraction in any binding window is at or below its reserve, its
  routes are excluded from automatic staffing with the reason `reserve: N% kept in the tank
  (D216)`, and `auto`'s ladder proceeds to the cheapest eligible metered route ... The
  reserve is a policy floor, distinct from the availability reader's health heuristics
  (`likely_exhausted` below 10%, which stays as a fail-safe). `bind` may still use a reserved
  channel only with `--authorized-by lee`. The staffing block always shows the reserve
  exclusion so Lee sees why a subscription was skipped. Named crews obey the same rule
  through their same-role fallbacks."

  Design notes (not prescriptive of exact field names — pick a clean shape following the
  existing schema's conventions, e.g. how `role_floors` or `never_automatic` are shaped as an
  array of cited records, or a default-plus-overrides shape; document your choice in the new
  field's schema description):

  - The reserve check belongs in `evaluate_eligibility` (`eligibility.py`), alongside the
    existing subscription-veto block (`channel.kind == "subscription" and headroom.health in
    _SUBSCRIPTION_VETO_HEALTH`) — add a second, distinct check using
    `headroom.remaining_fraction` compared against the channel's configured reserve fraction
    (falling back to the policy default when the channel has no explicit override). Every
    policy rule and reason must cite D216, exactly like the file's other cited rules.
  - `bind` mode's existing never-automatic authorization check in `staff.py`
    (`_staff_bind`, search for `NEVER_AUTOMATIC_REASON` and `BIND_AUTHORIZED_BY`) is the
    pattern to mirror for the reserve case: a route whose channel is at or below the reserve
    binds only with `--authorized-by lee`, refusing otherwise with a named error kind (do not
    reuse `never_automatic`'s error kind for a different reason).
  - The reserve is a **policy floor computed from `remaining_fraction`**, never a duplicate
    of the existing health-based veto — do not remove or weaken the existing
    `likely_exhausted` fail-safe; both checks run and either can independently exclude a
    route.
