## Stop / escalation condition

Stop and report back without guessing if a counted attempt's `verdict` shape is neither
`"pass"` nor `{"tier": "engine_validation"}` with `verified_success: true` (an unrecognized
pass shape — do not invent a third pass rule), or if `selection.reason`/`selection.excluded`
does not carry enough information to determine a reviewer-fallback line truthfully (report
that column as `unavailable` with the reason rather than guessing from route identity alone).
