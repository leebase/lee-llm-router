# Packet P5-1b — `evidence report`: explain an empty recommendation set; source rows per group

- Kind: `impl`
- Class: `impl/deterministic/none/s/python`
- Declared size: 3 files, at most 250 changed lines
- Owned paths: `src/lee_llm_router/staffing/evidence_report.py`,
  `tests/test_staffing_evidence_report.py`, `tests/test_doctor_evidence_report.py`
- Forbidden paths: everything else (`doctor.py`, `rollup.py`, `ladder_derivation.py`, config).
- Context: P5-1 landed `lee-llm-router evidence report --month YYYY-MM` (commit `45c9613`).
  The Phase 5 gate (sprint plan, gate 1) requires "route changes recommended by evidence with
  the evidence line" and "no number without a source row". Two gaps found by the Chief on the
  real ledger: (a) when `_route_changes` returns `[]` the text output omits the section
  entirely, so a reader cannot tell "no recommendation" from "not computed"; (b) group rows
  carry no pointer to the attempts they were computed from.

## Changes

1. **Empty recommendations are explained.** Make `_route_changes` also return, per class it
   examined, why no recommendation was produced, using exactly these reason strings:
   `fewer than 2 routes with evidence`, `fewer than 2 comparison-eligible routes (n>=MINIMUM_SAMPLE_SIZE)`,
   `fewer than 2 routes at pass_rate>=0.8`, `fewer than 2 routes with a usable marginal cost`,
   `cheapest route already first` (when the cheapest priced viable route is the only one or
   no other is more expensive). Keep the return shape backward compatible: return a dict
   `{"recommendations": [...same entries as today...], "not_recommended": [{"class_key", "reason"}]}`
   and update `build_evidence_report` to put `route_changes` (the list, unchanged) and a new
   `route_changes_not_recommended` (the list of `{class_key, reason}`) in the report. In
   `render_evidence_report`, always print the section: when non-empty, as today; when empty,
   `Recommended route changes: none` followed by one line per reason with the count of
   classes: `  <count> classes: <reason>` (sorted by count desc, then reason). Cite
   `MINIMUM_SAMPLE_SIZE` from `rollup`; do not restate 5.
2. **Source rows.** Every group in `classes` carries `source_attempt_ids` (the `attempt_id`
   of every record counted in the group, in ledger order) and `source_ledger` (the resolved
   ledger path string). In the text renderer add one line per group:
   `  source: <n> attempts from <ledger path> (<first three attempt_ids>, +<k> more)`
   (omit the `+k more` when three or fewer). Records without an `attempt_id` are counted and
   listed as `<no attempt_id>`.
